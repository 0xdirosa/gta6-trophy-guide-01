      Roadmap to the platinum ~200 hours · difficulty 4/10 · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies

      1. Stage 1 — Story & side missions 60–80 h

        Play the 5 chapters and answer every side call: purple icons and black icons with pink letters. Scan each new vehicle with WAiNK and study animals through binoculars as you go — 100% needs 200 of 277 vehicle models and 100 of 255 animals, and everything counts before the ending.

        ⚠ Do the balcony meditation with Lucia at Jason’s house in Chapter 1 — the one possibly missable trophy. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter: there is no mission replay.

      2. Stage 2 — 100% checklist & Challenges ~100 h

        After the ending, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is closer to 60% true completion: most side quests are not required and only 70 of the 80 Challenges are needed. What it demands in full: every robbery, underwater discovery, shooting range, drone race and Bret quest.

        ⚠ Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off this save.

      3. Stage 3 — Strangers & cleanup ~10 h

        Finish the Strangers & Freaks strands the checklist does not ask for (8 of 14), then mop up whatever is left: the contact finales, the money trophies and the odd one-offs. Every quest giver tied to a trophy is still around in free roam after the ending.

        ⚠ Avoid the Irredeemable criminal profile — it is permanent and makes the police more aggressive.

      ★ 1 possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot, starting with the Chapter 1 balcony. Everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.
